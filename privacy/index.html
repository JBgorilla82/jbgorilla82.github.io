YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

How Gorilla 82 handles the information you share when you get in touch.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This notice explains how Gorilla 82 uses the personal information you provide through the enquiry form on gorilla82.co.uk, and what your rights are under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who is responsible for your information

The data controller is John Beedles | Gorilla82.co.uk , trading as Gorilla 82, Dorset, UK.

Contact: info@gorilla82.co.uk

What information we collect

When you use the enquiry form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Your email address
  • The type of project you are interested in
  • Any details you choose to include in the optional project description

Please do not include sensitive personal information (such as health details) in your message. We do not need it.

How and why we use it

PurposeLawful basis
Reading and replying to your enquiry, and discussing a possible projectLegitimate interests (responding to people who contact us) and, where you are asking for a quote, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract
Preparing quotes and, if you go ahead, delivering the work and keeping recordsPerformance of a contract; legal obligation (accounting and tax records)
Keeping the website and form secure and preventing spamLegitimate interests

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not add you to a marketing mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with the service providers that help us receive and handle your enquiry:

  • FormSubmit, which processes the form submission and delivers it to us.
  • Gorilla82.co.uk & Google mail, which provides the info@gorilla82.co.uk mailbox.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or an adequacy arrangement.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Clients: project and invoicing records for up to 6 years, to meet accounting and tax requirements.

Cookies and tracking

This website [does not currently use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers]. If that changes, we will update this notice and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is inaccurate;
  • delete your information, in many circumstances;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • move your information to another provider, where this applies.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@gorilla82.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
