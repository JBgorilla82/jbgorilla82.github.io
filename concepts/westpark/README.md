# Westpark Carpentry & Building Services — private concept

A responsive single-page website concept for JB Digital Designs to present privately to Westpark Carpentry & Building Services.

## Preview
Open `index.html` in a browser. The layout is responsive and uses HTML/CSS with no build tools.

## Design direction
- Editorial, architectural feel rather than the repeated template used for earlier concepts.
- Warm off-white canvas, deep green typography, restrained leaf-green accents, and large asymmetrical project photography.
- Brand logo and project images are linked from Westpark's Checkatrade profile, so an internet connection is required to display them.
- Includes services, selected work, about, review proof point and enquiry CTA.

## Important before publishing
- This is an unsolicited private concept, not an approved official website.
- The logo and project images are linked from Westpark's Checkatrade profile. Get permission from the business before using these assets on a public or live website; replace with approved high-resolution assets if requested.
- Checkatrade profile content showed a 9.9/10 rating and 51 reviews when the concept was researched. Live page now may display updated figures; verify before launch.
- The concept links quote enquiries to Checkatrade because no direct business phone/email was confirmed. Replace with their approved contact details and a working enquiry form after permission.
- Service descriptions are based on their Checkatrade profile. Confirm service scope, locations, wording, experience claims, and current review count with the business before publication.
- External font loading requires internet access.
