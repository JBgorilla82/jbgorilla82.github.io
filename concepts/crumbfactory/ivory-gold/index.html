Good to know

The questions we hear most often.

How far ahead should I enquire?

As soon as you have your date. Popular dates fill early, and Sally will confirm availability when she replies.

How do I choose a collection?

Browse the Simplicity and Luxury collections for inspiration. Not sure which suits you? Tell us about your day and we will point you the right way.

Where do you deliver?

The Crumb Factory is based in Poole, Dorset. Include your venue in your enquiry and we will confirm delivery and set-up.

Can you cater for allergies and dietary needs?

Please mention any allergies or dietary requirements when you enquire so they can be planned from the start.

Can I ask for a custom design?

Yes. Every cake is designed around the couple it is made for, so bring your colours, flowers and ideas.
