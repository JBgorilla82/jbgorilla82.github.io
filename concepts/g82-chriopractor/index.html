FAQs

Questions answered

Does chiropractic treatment hurt?

Most patients find treatment comfortable and immediately relieving. You may feel mild soreness afterwards, similar to after exercise, which usually settles within a day or so.

Do I need a GP referral?

No referral is needed. You can book directly with us. If we think you need further tests or another specialist, we'll tell you.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition. Many patients feel improvement within a few visits. After your assessment we'll give you a clear plan and expected timeframe.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing is ideal. For massage, we use towels and professional draping so you always feel comfortable.

What's the difference between chiropractic care and sports massage?

Chiropractic focuses on joints, the spine and the nervous system, while sports massage works on muscles and soft tissue. Many patients benefit from both, which is why we offer them together.
