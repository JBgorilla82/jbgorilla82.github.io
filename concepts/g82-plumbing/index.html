FAQs

Common questions

How quickly can you get to an emergency?

We aim to reach most emergency callouts within two hours, day or night. When you call, we'll give you a realistic arrival time and talk you through making things safe in the meantime.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. All gas work, including boiler installs, repairs and servicing, is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers. You can ask to see our ID and licence number at any time.

Do you offer free quotes?

Absolutely. For larger jobs like bathrooms, boilers and heating systems we'll visit, assess and provide a clear fixed-price quote in writing, with no obligation.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. All workmanship is guaranteed, and new boilers and parts come with their manufacturer warranties. If anything isn't right, we'll come back and put it right.

Can you supply the bathroom or kitchen fittings?

We can design, supply and fit a complete project, or simply do the plumbing for fittings you've chosen yourself. Whatever suits your budget.
