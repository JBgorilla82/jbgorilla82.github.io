01 / A peaceful base

Farm cottages with space to breathe

G82 Holiday Rentals offers a small collection of self-catering cottages converted from original farm buildings. Each property has its own character — exposed beams, solid beds and fully equipped kitchens — while providing the comforts of a modern holiday home.

Whether you are planning a family break, a gathering of friends or a quiet escape for two, the cottages can be booked individually or together for larger groups of up to 24 guests.

04 / Explore Dorset

Coast, countryside
and local life

West Parley sits between the countryside and the coast, with Bournemouth, Poole and the Jurassic Coast all within easy reach.

Beaches

Bournemouth’s seven miles of sand, Sandbanks and the quieter stretches of the Jurassic Coast.

Walking

Gentle countryside paths, the South West Coast Path and nearby nature reserves.

Local pubs

Friendly village pubs within walking distance serving food and local ales.

Days out

Poole Harbour, Brownsea Island, Kingston Lacy and the wider Dorset landscape.
