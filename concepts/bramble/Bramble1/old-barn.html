Cottages / The Old Barn

The Old Barn

Sleeps 6 · 3 bedrooms

A large, airy and light main reception room with comfortable sofas for cosy nights in, and a TV and DVD player for entertainment. The kitchen and dining area seats up to six and has a newly refitted kitchen.

There is a walk-in shower room next to the stairs, which lead up to three bedrooms with feature beams and solid beds in each: one king size, one double and two singles. Upstairs there is also a modern bathroom with a shower bath.

At the rear of the property, a feature door opens out onto a south-facing patio area.
