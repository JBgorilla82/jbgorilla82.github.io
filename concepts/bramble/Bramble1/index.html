Our story

About us

Bramble Farm Cottages are family run holiday homes which have been up and running since 2014.

Our dream was to convert the old farm buildings into stylish and comfortable holiday rental cottages, each having their own individual style and original features and making the most of the rural setting in West Parley. We are close to Bournemouth Airport and the towns of Ferndown, Bournemouth and Poole. We offer accommodation for up to 24 people, perfect for weddings or large group bookings.

  • Free Wi-Fi is available in all of our cottages.
  • We regret that we do not take dogs or other pets in any of the holiday cottages.
  • Bookings are made through Airbnb, where you can check up-to-date availability.
