Cottages / The Dairy

The Dairy

Sleeps 8 · 3 bedrooms

The Dairy forms part of the original cow sheds on the farm and offers accommodation for up to eight guests.

Downstairs is a modern open-plan kitchen, diner and lounge with appliances including an under-counter fridge, oven, microwave and dishwasher, plus a lounge with ample seating. There is a walk-in shower room, a downstairs cloakroom, and a king size bedroom with French doors leading onto a patio area.

Upstairs there are two generously sized bedrooms with lots of light (please note there is restricted head height upstairs) and a separate dressing or storage room. There is ample parking.
