The Milk Churn

Sleeps 4 · 2 bedrooms

The Milk Churn forms part of the original cow sheds on the farm and offers accommodation for up to four guests.

Downstairs is a modern open-plan kitchen and diner with appliances including an under-counter fridge, oven, microwave and dishwasher, a separate lounge with ample seating, and a bathroom with a shower bath.

Upstairs there are two bedrooms with lots of light (please note there is restricted head height upstairs). There is ample parking, with a small patio to the front.
