Cottages / The Stable

The Stable

Sleeps 4 · 2 bedrooms

The Stable forms part of the original cow sheds on the farm and offers accommodation for up to four guests.

Downstairs is a modern open-plan lounge and diner with a separate kitchen (under-counter fridge, oven, microwave and dishwasher) and a bathroom with a shower bath. Two doors lead out to a side patio area.

Upstairs there are two bedrooms with lots of light (please note there is restricted head height upstairs). There is ample parking, with a small patio to the front.
