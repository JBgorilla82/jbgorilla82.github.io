West Parley & around

Local area

Bramble Farm Cottages sits in the village of West Parley, surrounded by countryside, with the coast and plenty of family attractions a short drive away.

Things to do nearby

When staying at Bramble Farm Cottages, not only are you surrounded by beautiful countryside but there are some lovely walks too. Down the end of the lane is a tranquil walk along the River Stour where you will see a variety of birds, swans and ducks. The quaint All Saints Church sits alongside the river and dates back to Saxon times. Why not try picking your own seasonal fruits at the PYO farm and explore the designated footpaths into the countryside both up and down the lane.

If you fancy something a little more adventurous, why not visit some of the tourist attractions nearby?

  • Parley Golf Centre

    A 9 hole course, pay and play, driving range, tuition, twilight golf and a café bar.

  • Aviation Museum

    A chance to see planes and helicopters up close and sit in them, in the grounds of Adventure Wonderland. It is only small, but well worth a visit for any plane fans.

  • Snowtrax Alpine Activity Centre

    A dry ski slope offering skiing, snowboarding, ski bobbing and ringos, with a menu, bar and children’s play area. The Alpine Adventure Park in the woodland at the bottom of the slope has an overhead cable ride, a wooden fort with bridges, slides and a fireman’s pole, swings, trampolines and a toddler area, with space for parents to relax.

  • Moors Valley

    A large outdoor activity centre in West Moors (Ferndown) with play trails, large and small play parks, a steam train ride around the park, picnic areas and a lake. There is a restaurant and snack bar on site, and bikes to hire to explore the grounds.

  • Go Ape (at Moors Valley)

    High ropes, crossings, tunnels, bridges and zip wire through the trees, with various packages on offer.

  • Altitude

    Similar to Go Ape but on a smaller scale. Suitable for over 5s, with low and high ropes, a power fan descender and an 11 metre climbing wall, in Littledown Park, which also has a play park, paddling pool, lake and small ride-on railway.

  • Castlepoint

    An undercover shopping centre with a wide variety of shops.

  • Bournemouth and Poole

    A short drive into the towns of Bournemouth and Poole offers a more comprehensive range of shops, restaurants and beautiful blue flag beaches.

Pubs and restaurants nearby

There are a selection of pubs within easy walking distance. Some are more geared for children than others.

  • The Curlew (Vintage Inns)

    Situated at the top of Church Lane, this pub offers a varied menu which caters for children. Beer garden at the front of the pub.

  • The Pear at Parley

    A popular pub with lovely big seating and a play area outside. Great food and a nice atmosphere, set within a large garden area.

  • The Owls Nest

    A family run pub which provides fresh homemade food. Aimed more at adults but will cater for children. No outside seating or children’s areas.

  • The Horns

    Offers a varied menu and has a big seating area outside. Caters for children.

The quiet town of Ferndown offers a range of Indian, Italian and pub restaurants as well as a range of takeaway establishments.
