YOUR COTTAGE

Room to settle in.

The Old Barn brings together the character of the original farm buildings with the space and comfort needed for a relaxed stay. The airy reception room is made for cosy evenings, while the kitchen and dining area provide space to gather. Upstairs, three bedrooms retain their feature beams. A private, enclosed south-facing patio offers a sunny spot outdoors.

At a glance

  • Sleeps up to 6 guests
  • 3 bedrooms: king, double and two single beds
  • Light, airy reception room and dining area
  • Private enclosed south-facing patio
  • Upstairs bathroom plus downstairs shower room
  • Free Wi-Fi
