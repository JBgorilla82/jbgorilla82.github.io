THE BRAMBLE FARM STORY

Made for moments
that matter.

Bramble Farm began with a family dream: to give the original farm buildings a new life as comfortable, individual holiday homes. Today, the five cottages offer a relaxed base in West Parley, with countryside on the doorstep and Dorset's coast and towns within reach.
