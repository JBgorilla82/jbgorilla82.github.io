GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might be wondering

How long does a website take? +

A simple one-page site can often be ready in around 1–2 days of build time. A 3–5 page website usually takes 2–3 days of build time. The overall timeline also depends on getting content, feedback and approvals.

Do I need to provide the content? +

You provide accurate business details and any photos you want included. I can help organise the wording, and more extensive copywriting or photography can be quoted separately.

Will it work on mobile? +

Yes. Responsive layouts are part of the build, so the site adapts to mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you help with an existing website? +

Yes. After reviewing the current site, I can recommend a focused refresh or a rebuild if that makes more sense. The first step is to understand what is and isn't working.

Is hosting included? +

Hosting and domain costs depend on the setup. Simple static websites can be hosted on platforms such as GitHub Pages, while custom domains and third-party form services may have separate costs. I'll explain the options before work starts.
